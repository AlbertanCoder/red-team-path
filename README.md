# Red Team Path

A free, audited path into AI red teaming, as a small web app with tick boxes that save.

**Open it:** https://albertancoder.github.io/red-team-path/

## The path

1. **Microsoft AI Red Teaming 101**: 10 short videos.
2. **One course day, then practice, for 21 days**: the SecurityElites course "AI Red Teaming From Zero", then Gray Swan Arena's training sets. Five write-ups along the way.
3. **Python**: the first five sections of freeCodeCamp's Python Certification.
4. **Bounties**: Mozilla 0DIN, then an application to Anthropic's model safety bug bounty.

Everything is free and runs in a web browser.

## What the app does

- **Next** shows the one thing to do next, and why.
- **Time today** (1, 2 or 4 hours) sets how much is on your plate. Nothing is skipped at any setting: at 1 hour the course and the practice take turns.
- **Path** lists every box, each with a link to its page. Ticks save in the browser by themselves.
- **Copy my progress** gives a code you can load on another device.
- **Words** explains about 40 terms in plain English, with a "Test me" switch that hides the meanings.
- **Jobs** lists real job titles, posted pay, what the ads asked for and where to look. Ads were read on 16 and 17 Nov 2026.
- **Info** holds the reasons for each place, the rules, a write-up template and the audit: every claim, whether it was checked against its source, and what was corrected.
- Dark and light themes, and a streak counter.

## Put it on an iPhone home screen

1. Open the link in Safari.
2. Tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.

Ticks are stored on the device you tick them on. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## Files

- `index.html`, `style.css`, `app.js`: the whole app. No build step and no libraries.
- `icons/`: the app icon.
- The path, the reasons and the audit all live at the top of `app.js`.
