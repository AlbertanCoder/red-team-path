Home is the path as one list. Tap the lab, read, tap Back. 2. One thing per screen
No lists. Each screen holds one thing and a Next button. 3. Three tabs and a lab grid
Today, Labs, More. All 12 labs on one grid. The lab page folds up. 4. Opens on your lab (recommended)
No home screen to pass through. The app opens on the lab you are doing.
